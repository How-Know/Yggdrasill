Homework Live Activity — Xcode target status

The HomeworkLiveActivity Widget Extension is already included in
Runner.xcodeproj and embedded in Runner.

Required identifiers:
- Extension bundle ID:
  com.beleunu.yggdrasillStudent.HomeworkLiveActivity
- Shared App Group:
  group.com.beleunu.yggdrasillStudent
- URL scheme:
  yggstudent

Do not create the target again. When moving to another Mac, use the checked-in
Runner.xcodeproj and let Automatic Signing provision both Runner and the
extension.

Live Activities require a physical device. iPhone needs iOS 16.1 or later;
iPad lock-screen Live Activities require iPadOS 17 or later. Windows Flutter
run cannot display them.
